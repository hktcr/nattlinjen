# Nattlinjen — whole-game virtual player review

## Question and acceptance target
Assess and improve the existing Swedish story-and-mathematics game as a coherent playable world. The owner's requested scope is all eight chapters, 24 challenges, narrative, graphics, tension, experience, help, saved progression and accessibility. Tasks should be performed through meaningful world objects/actions, not generic web-form cards on an image. Narrative remains legible, paced text authored directly on backdrops. Exact mathematical entry is appropriate where it improves clarity; do not replace clarity with artificial fictional controls.

This is a virtual, AI-agent player panel. No actual children or disabled participants have been recruited. Persona reactions are critical hypotheses, not empirical evidence of human enjoyment, learning or disability access.

## Review process and scope limits
Preflight first: each proposed role independently states missing competence, duplication and critical limits. Do not begin R1 until a distinct start signal follows preflight consolidation. R1 contributions go in separate files and must not read other R1 outputs. Then cross-pollination, explicit strongest objections, dispositions, fixes and exact-revision retests. Root coordinates concurrency waves; no claim of simultaneous review when roles are paused.
Use stable VEP IDs assigned centrally at merge and stable feedback IDs. Preserve disagreements. A finding is implemented and retested, rejected with reason, or explicitly open—not silently omitted.
The runtime writer does not act as an independent guardian. Process guardian and two separate factual guardians (mathematics/content and state/function/privacy) remain independent of player-lens opinions.
